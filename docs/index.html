Work order triage and vendor follow-up

PM

Maintenance requests sit unassigned and vendors go quiet until a tenant complains.

  1. Trigger: tenant submits a maintenance request in AppFolio or Buildium
  2. AI reads the request and tags it emergency, urgent, or routine by trade
  3. Work order is assigned to the preferred vendor for that trade and property
  4. Python script checks open orders daily and emails vendors who haven't updated
  5. Tenant gets status updates; manager sees a short daily list of stuck orders

Outcome: no work order goes untouched, and emergencies reach a human first.

Delinquency and five-day notices

PM

Late rent tracking and notice prep eat the first week of every month.

  1. Trigger: grace period ends on the scheduled date each month
  2. Delinquency report is pulled and balances are checked against the tenant ledger
  3. Friendly reminder goes out first by email and SMS
  4. Five-day notices are generated from a template with tenant, unit, and amount filled in
  5. Manager approves the batch; serving and dates are logged per tenant

Outcome: notices go out on time and every one is documented for court if needed.

Month-end owner statements

PM

Statements go out late because reconciliation errors surface at the last minute.

  1. Trigger: month-end close begins
  2. Bank feed is checked against the ledger; unmatched items are listed
  3. Script flags duplicate entries, miscoded expenses, and unexplained balances
  4. After review, owner statements and management fees are generated
  5. Each owner receives a statement packet with a short plain-English summary

Outcome: errors are caught before owners see them, not after.

AI voice agent for inbound calls

PM

Calls after hours or during showings go to voicemail and leads go cold.

  1. Trigger: a prospect, tenant, or owner calls the office line
  2. Voice agent answers, identifies the caller type, and handles common questions
  3. Prospects are pre-qualified and offered a showing time
  4. Maintenance emergencies are routed to the on-call number immediately
  5. Call summary and transcript land in the CRM with a follow-up task

Outcome: every call is answered and logged, day or night.

Contract to close timeline

TC

Key dates live inside PDFs and get re-typed by hand for every file.

  1. Trigger: executed contract arrives in Dotloop or by email
  2. AI extracts parties, price, and every contract deadline
  3. TC reviews the extracted dates, then they become calendar events with reminders
  4. Intro emails go to buyer, seller, lender, and title with the timeline attached
  5. Reminders fire before inspection, appraisal, financing, and closing deadlines

Outcome: the file is fully set up the day it goes under contract.

Instant lead response

TC

Website leads wait hours for a reply and book with whoever answers first.

  1. Trigger: someone submits a form on the agent's website
  2. Make.com creates or updates the contact in GoHighLevel with source tags
  3. Personalized text and email go out within a minute
  4. Agent gets a task with the lead's details and a booking link
  5. Unresponsive leads move into a nurture sequence automatically

Outcome: every lead hears back fast, even when the agent is in a showing.
